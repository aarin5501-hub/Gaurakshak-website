Mastitis warning signs

Signs that should trigger closer observation and, when persistent, veterinary assessment.

⚠
Pay attention
Observe the animal, udder and milk together. A single sign does not establish a diagnosis.
◉Udder swelling
♨Unusual udder heat
◌Pain / tenderness during milking
🥛Flakes, clots, watery milk or colour change
↓Reduced milk yield vs baseline
◒Fever or systemic signs

What to do

1
Observe and record.Compare with the animal's usual baseline.
2
Repeat validated testing when indicated.Consider SCC and other validated milk-quality tests.
3
Contact a veterinarian when needed.Especially for persistent abnormalities or systemic signs.
